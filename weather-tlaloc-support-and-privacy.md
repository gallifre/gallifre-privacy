## Privacy Policy

Weather Tlaloc is a macOS weather wallpaper app. It turns local weather conditions into visual desktop modes such as clear weather, umbrella weather, and heavier rain.

### Information We Collect

Weather Tlaloc may request your approximate location. This location is used only to request local weather data and choose the appropriate wallpaper mode.

Weather Tlaloc does not collect account information, payment information, contact lists, photos, messages, advertising identifiers, or analytics identifiers.

### How Location Is Used

Location is used for app functionality only:

- Requesting local weather data through Apple WeatherKit.
- Choosing the visual wallpaper mode for current weather conditions.
- Requesting fallback forecast data from Open-Meteo if Apple Weather is unavailable.

Location data is not used for tracking, advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

### Local Storage

Weather Tlaloc stores app settings locally on your Mac, such as selected theme, refresh interval, last refresh time, and selected artwork identifier. This local data is used to keep the app working consistently between launches.

Weather Tlaloc does not store your precise coordinates as user-facing history.

### Third-Party Services

Weather Tlaloc uses Apple WeatherKit for weather data. If Apple Weather is unavailable, the app may request fallback forecast data from Open-Meteo using the same approximate location.

Weather Tlaloc does not include third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

### Data Sharing

Weather Tlaloc does not sell your data. Weather Tlaloc does not share your data for advertising or tracking.

### Children's Privacy

Weather Tlaloc does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

### Your Choices

You can disable location access for Weather Tlaloc at any time in macOS System Settings. If location or weather data is unavailable, the app shows fallback visual guidance instead of blocking normal use.

To remove locally stored app settings, delete the app and its local app data from your Mac.

## 隐私政策

Weather Tlaloc 是一款 macOS 天气壁纸应用。它会把本地天气转化为桌面视觉模式，例如晴朗天气、适合带伞的天气和较强降雨天气。

### 我们收集的信息

Weather Tlaloc 可能会请求你的大致位置。该位置只用于请求本地天气数据，并选择合适的壁纸模式。

Weather Tlaloc 不收集账号信息、支付信息、通讯录、照片、消息、广告标识符或分析标识符。

### 位置信息的用途

位置信息仅用于应用功能：

- 通过 Apple WeatherKit 请求本地天气数据。
- 根据当前天气选择对应的视觉壁纸模式。
- 如果 Apple Weather 不可用，使用同一组大致位置向 Open-Meteo 请求备用天气预报。

位置信息不会用于追踪、广告、画像分析，也不会出售给第三方。

### 本地存储

Weather Tlaloc 会在你的 Mac 本地保存应用设置，例如已选择的主题、刷新间隔、上次刷新时间和已选择的作品标识。这些本地数据用于让应用在多次启动之间保持一致体验。

Weather Tlaloc 不会把你的精确坐标保存为面向用户的位置历史。

### 第三方服务

Weather Tlaloc 使用 Apple WeatherKit 获取天气数据。如果 Apple Weather 不可用，应用可能会使用同一组大致位置向 Open-Meteo 请求备用天气预报。

Weather Tlaloc 不包含第三方广告 SDK、第三方分析 SDK 或追踪 SDK。

### 数据共享

Weather Tlaloc 不出售你的数据，也不会为了广告或追踪目的共享你的数据。

### 儿童隐私

Weather Tlaloc 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

### 你的选择

你可以随时在 macOS 系统设置中关闭 Weather Tlaloc 的位置权限。如果位置或天气数据不可用，应用会显示备用视觉提示，而不是阻止正常使用。

如需删除本地保存的应用设置，可以删除应用及其在 Mac 上的本地应用数据。
