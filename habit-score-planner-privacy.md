# Habit Score Planner Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

Habit Score Planner is a local-first habit tracker for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information for advertising, analytics, profiling, or sale. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

## On-device data

Habits, completion history, reminders, settings, and local backups are stored on the device. They remain under the user's control and can be removed by deleting the app or its data.

## Optional iCloud sync

If the user has iCloud enabled and permits the app to sync, habit data is stored in the user's private CloudKit database managed by Apple. This transfer is optional and is controlled by the user's iCloud account and device settings. Habit Score Planner does not operate its own server for this data.
