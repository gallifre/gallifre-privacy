# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

GalliFre respects your privacy.

## Information We Collect

GalliFre is designed to work primarily on your device. We do not collect personal information directly through our own servers.

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

- item names
- usage records
- stock and reminder settings
- forecast-related data
- language and appearance preferences

## How Information Is Used

Your data is used only to provide app features such as:

- tracking household stock
- recording usage
- generating forecast results
- managing reminders and settings

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

## Data Storage

App data is mainly stored locally on your device. If you enable Apple services such as iCloud backup, your data may be included in those services under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

GalliFre does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
