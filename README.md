## English

**Effective date:** May 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Restock / 囤货管家** app ("the App") handles your information.

### 1. Summary

**Restock is a fully offline application.** We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

### 2. Information We Do **Not** Collect

We do not collect:

- Personal identifiers (name, email, phone, IP address, device ID)
- Location data
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Crash reports (no crash-reporting SDK is integrated)
- Advertising identifiers (we do not show ads)

### 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores the following information **only on your device**, using Apple's standard `UserDefaults` and local file system:

- Items you create (name, unit, optional photo or emoji, optional pack size)
- Inventory records and transactions you log
- Optional barcodes you bind to items
- App preferences (theme, language override, threshold values)

This data **never leaves your device**. It is not backed up to any server we operate. If you have iCloud Backup enabled, Apple may include the App's data in your iCloud backup; that backup is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not ours.

### 4. Camera Usage

We request camera access **only when you tap "Scan barcode" or "Take photo"**. Captured images are stored locally in the App's data store. We do not upload them anywhere.

### 5. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only network operations are made by Apple's frameworks themselves (e.g., font rendering for emoji), not by us.

### 6. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, the App is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

### 7. Your Rights

To delete all data the App stores about you, simply delete the App from your device. iOS will remove all locally stored data automatically.

### 8. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice.

### 9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at:

**starlight.ripple@gmail.com**
