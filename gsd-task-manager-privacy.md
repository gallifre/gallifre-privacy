# GSD Task Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

GSD Task Manager is an offline-first task manager for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy applies to the version distributed by YABO WANG under the bundle identifier `com.gallifre.gsd`.

## Data we collect

GSD Task Manager does not collect, sell, or share personal data for advertising or analytics. We do not operate an account service for the app's default local workflow, and we do not receive the tasks, notes, tags, reminders, or files that you create in the app.

## Where your data is stored

Tasks and settings are stored locally on your device. The app can export your task data when you choose to do so. Optional sync features, if enabled by you, may transmit the data required by that service; those transfers are initiated by you and are not required to use the core app.

## Permissions and system services

The app may use Apple's system services, such as notifications, widgets, the share sheet, and iCloud or other system sync features, only when you enable or invoke them. Apple may process information as described in Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

## Children

GSD Task Manager does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. Questions about this policy or the app can be raised through the public issue tracker at https://github.com/gallifre/gallifre-privacy/issues.

This policy describes the app distributed by YABO WANG. It is not the privacy policy of the upstream GSD project or any third-party sync provider.
